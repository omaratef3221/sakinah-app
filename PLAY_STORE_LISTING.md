Transform your spiritual journey with Sakinah, the ultimate Islamic habit tracker designed to help you build lasting positive habits and strengthen your connection with Allah.

**KEY FEATURES**

🕌 Prayer Times & Reminders
• Accurate prayer times based on your location
• Dubai calculation method (18-degree horizon)
• Never miss Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, or Isha

📿 Smart Habit Tracking
• Track daily Islamic habits like Quran recitation, dhikr, and Sunnah practices
• Set personalized goals and streaks
• Visual progress with beautiful charts and statistics
• Custom habit categories: Worship, Knowledge, Character, Health, and more

🤲 Dua of the Day
• Daily curated duas from authentic Islamic sources
• Arabic text with English transliteration and translation
• Comprehensive dua collection for various occasions
• References from Quran and Hadith

📖 Islamic Resources
• Complete Athkar collection (morning/evening remembrance)
• Umrah and Hajj step-by-step guides
• Easy access to essential Islamic knowledge

🧭 Qibla Compass
• Find the direction of Qibla anywhere in the world
• Accurate GPS-based calculation
• Beautiful, intuitive interface

✨ Premium Design
• Modern, elegant Islamic-inspired interface
• Dark/Light mode support
• Smooth animations and beautiful gradients
• Clean, distraction-free experience

📊 Progress Tracking
• View your habit completion rates
• Track streaks and milestones
• Analyze your spiritual growth over time
• Stay motivated with achievement insights

🌍 Location-Aware
• Automatic location detection
• Customizable prayer time calculation methods
• Works offline after initial setup

**WHY SAKINAH?**

Sakinah means "tranquility" and "peace of mind" in Arabic - the very state we seek through consistent Islamic practices. Our app combines modern technology with timeless Islamic wisdom to help you:

• Build consistent daily habits aligned with Islamic teachings
• Never miss your prayers with accurate, location-based times
• Learn and memorize authentic duas for every situation
• Track your spiritual progress with meaningful insights
• Create a balanced routine of worship, learning, and self-improvement

**PERFECT FOR**

• Muslims seeking to strengthen their daily practice
• Those wanting to build consistency in worship
• Anyone looking to track Islamic habits systematically
• Individuals preparing for Ramadan, Hajj, or Umrah
• Parents teaching children about Islamic routines
